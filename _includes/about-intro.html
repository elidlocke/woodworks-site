About Us

Since 2004, Woodworks Refinishing has provided quality hardwood floor refinishing services to homeowners, landlords, businesses and school boards in Kitchener, Waterloo, Cambridge and across the Waterloo Region.

After gaining experience with a variety of custom hardwood installers and refinishers across Canada, Ben Locke founded Woodworks Hardwood Refinishing. Locke’s portfolio includes all scales of hardwood projects, from residential apartments to custom gymnasiums.

Woodworks is a Bona certified contractor. We use state-of-the-art dustless sanding equipment, tailored to the size of the job, so the refinishing process leaves as little dust and waste in your home as possible.

Ben Locke personally participates in every job that Woodworks Refinishing completes, assuring the highest standard of quality for hardwood floor refinishing in Kitchener-Waterloo and Cambridge.
