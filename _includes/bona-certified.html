A Bona Certified Craftsman

An invitation-only standard for hardwood floor professionals

By Invitation Only

Bona doesn't certify every contractor who asks. The Bona Certified Craftsman program is by invitation only, open to a select group of flooring professionals whose work, reputation and business practices meet Bona's standards. In Bona's own words, a Certified Craftsman "combines skill, passion and trustworthiness," so homeowners know they are working with a trained and certified professional.

Trained by Bona in Toronto

Ben travelled to Toronto to train directly with Bona, learning their sanding, finishing and dust-containment systems from the people who make them. Certified Craftsmen are held to Bona's top standards for workmanship, product application and customer care, and the certification has to be kept up, not just earned once.

Why We Finish with Bona

Bona has focused on wood floors since 1919. It's a family-owned Swedish company, and its water-based finishes are used by floor professionals around the world. Those finishes are low-odour and dry quickly, so you can get back into your rooms sooner. They also give a hard-wearing, crystal-clear coat that won't yellow the way older oil-based finishes do. Paired with Bona's dust-containment sanding, it means a better floor with far less mess in your home.
