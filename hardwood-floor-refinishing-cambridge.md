Cambridge brings together three historic towns, Galt, Preston and Hespeler, and each has older homes with hardwood floors worth saving. Woodworks Hardwood Refinishing has been sanding and refinishing hardwood floors in Cambridge since 2004, and owner Ben Locke works on every job himself.

As a **[Bona Certified Craftsman](/bona-certified-craftsman/)** using a dustless sanding process, we keep your home clean while we bring your floors back to life.

## Cambridge homes we refinish

### Stone and brick century homes in Galt

Galt has some of the oldest housing in the region, including stone and solid brick homes near the Grand River and downtown. Floors in these homes may be original pine or hardwood, often face-nailed, uneven, or sanded several times over the past century. We assess how much wood is left before we start, set any protruding nails, and sand only as much as needed. Where boards are too damaged to save, we replace them with reclaimed wood to keep the floor's character.

### Preston and Hespeler period homes

Preston and Hespeler have many well-kept 19th- and early 20th-century homes, along with smaller period houses that are popular with first-time buyers. Many of these have original hardwood that's dull, worn or hidden under carpet. Refinishing it is one of the most affordable ways to add character and value to an older home.

### Post-war bungalows and storey-and-a-halfs

Cambridge's post-war neighbourhoods are full of solid 1940s and 1950s houses with narrow-strip oak floors. A sand and finish, sometimes with a modern stain, can make these homes feel new again.

### Newer subdivisions

Newer houses across Cambridge often have prefinished or engineered hardwood. Prefinished floors can usually be refreshed with a buff and coat or fully sanded. Engineered floors can usually be sanded once, after we confirm the real-wood top layer is thick enough.
