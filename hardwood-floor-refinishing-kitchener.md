## Kitchener homes we refinish

### Century homes near downtown

Neighbourhoods around downtown Kitchener, like Victoria Park, Mount Hope–Breithaupt and Cherry Park, have many homes built in the late 1800s and early 1900s. Their original floors are often oak, maple or pine, and many have been sanded a few times already. These floors need a careful touch: we sand only as deep as necessary to save as much of the original wood as possible, and we can replace damaged boards with reclaimed hardwood that matches the age and colour of the floor.

### Wartime and post-war houses

After the Second World War, whole neighbourhoods of small "victory homes" went up in Kitchener, and the St. Mary's area is even protected as a heritage district because of them. Many of these storey-and-a-half houses and the bungalows that followed in the 1950s have their original strip hardwood hidden under decades of carpet or vinyl. Uncovering and refinishing it is one of the best-value upgrades you can make to an older Kitchener home.

### Lofts and condos

Kitchener's old factories have been turned into loft condos, and many newer condos downtown and around the city have hardwood or engineered hardwood. Condo jobs mean working around elevator bookings, building work hours and neighbours. Our dustless equipment keeps hallways and units clean. If your floor is engineered, we check the thickness of the real-wood top layer first to make sure it can be safely sanded.

### Rentals and investment properties

For landlords and property managers, refinishing hardwood between tenants is often cheaper than replacing flooring and makes a unit more appealing to renters. A buff and coat can refresh a floor in good condition in a day, while a full sand and finish brings back badly worn floors.
