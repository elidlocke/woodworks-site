## Waterloo homes we refinish

### Heritage homes around Uptown

The MacGregor-Albert neighbourhood beside Uptown is Waterloo's oldest and is a designated heritage district. Along with older areas like Mary Allen and Westmount, it has many homes built from the late 1800s to the 1920s. Floors in these homes are often original oak, maple or pine, sometimes with gaps, old repairs or several earlier sandings. We take a careful, minimal approach to keep as much original wood as possible, and we use reclaimed hardwood for repairs so patches blend in.

### Post-war and mid-century houses

Waterloo grew quickly after the Second World War, and many modest wartime-era and 1950s houses still have their original strip hardwood under carpet or vinyl. Pulling up the carpet and refinishing the wood underneath is one of the most cost-effective upgrades for these homes.

### Family homes from the 1970s onward

Many Waterloo homes from the 1970s through the 2000s have hardwood in living rooms, dining rooms and upstairs bedrooms, often with carpet in the halls or on the stairs. As one of our Waterloo customers found, we can install matching hardwood where the carpet was, then sand and stain everything together so the whole floor looks like one piece.

### Lofts, condos and student rentals

Converted factories and warehouses near Uptown, and the many condos and rental units around the universities, often have hardwood or engineered hardwood. For landlords, refinishing between tenants is far cheaper than replacing flooring. For engineered floors, we always check the thickness of the real-wood top layer before sanding.
